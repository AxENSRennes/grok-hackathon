Ten character families

10 distinct sheets × 5 characters. Studies of small, compact humans with large heads, simple hairstyles, and clothing. The sheets are art-direction references generated with the built-in imagegen tool; they are not yet production sprites. No visual choice was final at the time of this exploration.

Ochre, sage, and denim; compact humans with brown outlines, the most detailed version.
