# Sidequest Live — Hackathon question bank

Drafted for the Grok Bot Community Engineering London Hackathon on 22 October 2026.
Alternate playful icebreakers and useful audience questions. These are opinion/self-report polls, not knowledge quizzes. No answer is marked correct.

## Host notes

- Start with 1–4: a topical joke, a need, a relatable confession, and an opportunity to help.
- For a short demo, use 1, 2, 3 and 8.
- Keep the atmosphere playful; vote positions express opinions and current needs.
- Suggested PHP follow-up line: "Somewhere, a WordPress site just felt that."
- Suggested code-explanation follow-up line: "The left side is writing the README. The right side is asking their agent what a README is."
- Suggested demo-readiness follow-up line: "Confidence is high. Evidence is still loading."
