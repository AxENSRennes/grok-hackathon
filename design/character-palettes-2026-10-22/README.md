# Ten character families

10 distinct sheets, 5 characters per sheet, generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. Exploration only; no product code was changed.

Open `index.html` to compare the ten sheets. Full prompts and source paths are in `prompts.json`.

- **01: Warm**: Ochre, sage, and denim; compact humans with brown outlines, the most detailed version. File: `01-warm-pocket-humans.png`.
- **02: Candy**: Bright pastel colors and plum outlines; large hairstyles and short bodies. File: `02-candy-bean-people.png`.
- **03: Capsule humans**: Very rounded silhouettes, oversized hoodies, tiny feet; closest to a human/bean blend. File: `03-pocket-capsule-humans.png`.
- **04: Retro neighborhood**: Muted colors and dark outlines; small RPG resident silhouettes. File: `04-old-handheld-neighborhood.png`.
- **05: Soft pastels**: Soft colored outlines; peach, sage, and lilac clothing with a warm Gather feel. File: `05-soft-outline-community.png`.
- **06: Arcade**: Cyan, hot pink, yellow, and violet; contrasting silhouettes and subtle accessories. File: `06-arcade-community-night.png`.
- **07: Ultra simple**: Large pixels, flat shapes, and very few details; best direction for very small avatars. File: `07-twelve-pixel-club.png`.
- **08: Boxy hoodies**: Wider heads and clothing; generous shoes and sleeves, a more illustrated style. File: `08-boxy-hoodie-humans.png`.
- **09: Small and round**: Round faces and soft proportions; cream, apricot, sage, and pale blue palette. File: `09-soft-round-pocket-people.png`.
- **10: Bold crew**: Black outlines and bold colors; characters are easy to distinguish on a map. File: `10-bold-pocket-crew.png`.

Recorded visual review: exactly five characters on each of the ten sheets. All are original, with human faces, hairstyles, clothing, short feet, and compact proportions. Detail density varies deliberately; some images remain more detailed than the intended low-resolution grid. These are style references to simplify after selection, not animation-ready sprite sheets.
